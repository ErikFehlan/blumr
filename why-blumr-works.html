Why blumr Works

Recruiters are often engulfed by hundreds of resumes for a single job posting—creating one critical challenge: which candidates are actually worth spending time on?

blumr cuts through the noise by evaluating every candidate against the requirements of the role, the hiring manager’s priorities, and the feedback gathered throughout the search. Instead of relying on keywords or gut instinct alone, recruiters get a clearer picture of who fits, why they fit, and where potential concerns may exist.

And because blumr is self-sharpening, every input, decision, and result helps improve the accuracy of future assessments. The more you use it, the better it understands what a strong candidate looks like for you.

Less time reviewing resumes. More time engaging the right people.
