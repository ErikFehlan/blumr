the owner's tax adviser. Current Ohio guidance distinguishes physical presence
in Ohio from an out-of-state seller. The county vendor-license application fee

page says paper processing can take up to six weeks.

city name or choose a remote-seller route merely because sales are online.

## After an actual registration is issued

1. Verify jurisdiction, active status, effective date and filing frequency from
   the authority's record; retain identifiers privately.
2. Add the genuine registration to the matching live Stripe account. This step
   alone does not register the LLC with the authority.
3. Verify an address-based calculation with the approved SaaS code and exclusive
   price. Inspect `taxability_reason`; explain zero tax. `not_collecting` is not
   successful readiness verification. Do not make a real charge for this check.
4. Assign the return-filing owner and record actual deadlines. Review other
   locations/jurisdictions, then approve the tax-readiness flag separately.
5. Keep checkout closed until the separate release decision.
