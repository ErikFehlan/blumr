ABOUT BLUMR

Built by recruiters.
For recruiters.

Recruiting is full of information. The real work is knowing what matters.

blumr brings resumes, role requirements, and hiring-manager feedback into one clear candidate picture. It turns scattered details into focused assessments, better questions, and concise submittals—so you can spend less time piecing things together and more time connecting with people.

YOUR EXPERTISE KEEPS IT GROWING

Your insight makes it sharper.

blumr is self-sharpening. The inputs you add, the reasoning you review, and the outcomes you record help refine its understanding of each candidate and what matters for the role.

As that context grows, the picture becomes more informed. Your experience shapes the assessment. Your judgment makes the final call.
